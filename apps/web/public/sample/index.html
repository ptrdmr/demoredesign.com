Sample · Group & Community Revenue System

A repeatable system for turning Whittier's schools, teams, seniors, and local groups into standing bookings.

This sample engagement hands the marketing team a research-backed area audit, a curated partner map of real local organizations, and a copy-paste outreach playbook, sequenced into a 90-day launch path.

Prepared by Peter DeMore · Neighborhood Bookings · June 2026 · Whittier, CA

Executive Summary

The situation, the opportunity, and what this plan delivers, in brief. Full market evidence lives in the Area Audit.

The situation. This sample is built for a fast food chain in Whittier, a market with strong group-booking potential. Whittier is a large, stable, family-heavy community of ~85,800 residents in ~28,100 households averaging 3.01 people each, well above the LA County (2.81) and U.S. (2.59) norms, with 73% family households. Inside the city sit three K–12 districts and two colleges enrolling well over 40,000 students, a growing 65+ cohort of ~12,500–12,900, and active local community groups along Whittier Blvd.

The opportunity. New fast-casual chains at The Groves are intensifying competition for ordinary dinner traffic. A fast food location should not fight on that ground. Its edge is the group experience and the daytime / off-peak occasion that chains don't serve well, where the location already has open capacity. The market is mature and slightly shrinking (−3.5% since 2020), which means the winning play is retention and recurring relationships, not chasing new-resident churn.

What this plan delivers. A repeatable outreach system, not a one-time campaign, three working tools plus an integrated 90-day launch plan that sequences them into "do this first, then this."

Attack These Four Segments First

These four combine the highest recurring value, the easiest access to a decision-maker, and the tightest fit with a fast food chain's off-peak goals. Everything else, schools, the Chamber, nonprofits, faith groups, colleges, and city camps, follows in a deliberate sequence. Full detail in the Partner Map.

1. Service Clubs

#1

Whittier Rotary, Sunrise Rotary, Host Lions, Kiwanis, standing weekly/biweekly meetings mean instant recurring covers, a single easy decision-maker, and breakfast clubs that hit your deadest hours directly.

Fastest, highest-ROI win.

2. Senior Centers & 55+

#2

Uptown Senior Center, Whispering Fountains, Altura at The Groves. Fast food fits familiar, budget-friendly group outings, they travel in organized groups and book recurring weekday-morning outings into your slowest windows.

Note: Parnell Park Senior Center is closed for renovation through 2027.

3. Youth Sports Leagues

#3

AYSO Region 75, Whittier PONY, Murphy Ranch Little League, Whittier Girls Softball. Multi-month seasons of weekly post-game meals and opening-day breakfasts create a compounding "team spot" habit.

Lock deals before fall season opens.

4. Car Clubs & Local Events

#4

Cruising Uptown Whittier, Classic Cruisin. A recurring community or car-club night can drive evening traffic and daytime return visits if parking and permits work.

Confirm permits/parking before launch.

The 90-Day Launch Plan

The integrated quick-start that sequences the Audit, Map, and Playbook into a concrete order of operations. Before anything ships, confirm every item in the checklist below with the venue owner.

  1. Phase 0 · Setup (Days 1–7)

    Confirm all pricing/flags below; build the tracker (7 stages + columns); finalize bilingual flyer + brand assets; personalize script templates with venue pricing.

    Target: a loaded toolkit, nothing sent on assumptions
  2. Phase 1 · Fast Wins (Weeks 1–4)

    Attend a Sunrise Rotary meeting and a Whittier Host Lions meeting as a guest; call the Uptown Senior Center to schedule a recurring "Sunrise Senior Club" breakfast; email AYSO 75, Whittier PONY, and Murphy Ranch LL boards before fall season; Explore a community night with Cruising Uptown Whittier or Classic Cruisin; join the Whittier Area Chamber and book a "Wake Up Whittier."

    Target: 15–20 prospects contacted; 3–5 first bookings; 1–2 recurring arrangements secured
  3. Phase 2 · Build the Pipeline (Weeks 5–8)

    Run the full 5-touch cadence on the Phase 1 list; layer in Youth Nonprofits for summer field trips; open K–12 PTAs/Athletic Directors with Spirit Night and team-meal pitches; leverage the Chamber relationship for warm intros.

    Target: 40+ prospects contacted; 6–10 first bookings/month; ≥40% converting to recurring
  4. Phase 3 · Deepen & Diversify (Weeks 9–13)

    Convert Booked → Recurring with the "Host of the Month" perk; add Faith Groups, Private Schools, Colleges, and City Camps; collect testimonials and referrals; run the weekly 30-minute pipeline review.

    Target: 8–12 active recurring groups by end of Q1

Inputs Needed From the Venue

The component documents deliberately use smart defaults, not invented venue promotions. Every figure flagged Confirm below must be set by the venue owner before the team sends a single message.

  1. 1Spirit-night / dine-to-donate %, modeled at 15–20%; confirm the real rate, whether takeout/online counts, and any headcount minimum.
  2. 2Per-person group pricing, team bundles, camper/scout lunch, senior breakfast price, college rates.
  3. 3Average check size, needed to estimate value per visit/group in the tracker.
  4. 4Off-peak capacity windows, exact slowest days/times (we assumed weekday breakfast + lunch are most open).
  5. 5Group capacity / max headcount and reservation lead time.
  6. 6Parking-lot event feasibility, lot size, city permits, parking, evening staffing.
  7. 7Catering capability, menu, minimums, delivery radius for local-business drops.
  8. 8Brand assets, logo, photos, and a fundraiser flyer template.
  9. 9Past group specials & past bookings, once provided, we replace smart defaults with proven, real offers.
  10. 10Organizer perk budget, what the venue owner will comp for "Host of the Month" / loyalty.

Next Steps

Three moves to activate this plan

1. Delivery. This plan and its three component files, plus this hosted web version and PDF copies, are delivered as the complete engagement package within 2–3 weeks of approval.

2. Stakeholder meeting. Schedule a working session with the venue owner to confirm the inputs above, green-light the four priority segments, and assign an owner for the tracker and weekly pipeline review.

3. Decide on ongoing support. Determine whether the venue wants periodic check-ins, monthly KPI review, target-list refreshes, seasonal campaign tune-ups, as an ongoing engagement, or will run the system in-house from here.
