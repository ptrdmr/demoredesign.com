# Group & Community Revenue System — Offer

## Who this is for

Independent restaurants, diners, bowling centers, bar-grills, and family entertainment centers with empty weekday or off-peak dayparts. You have capacity. You need recurring local groups — schools, teams, seniors, clubs, employers — not another one-off promo.

---

## Core offer — $5,000

**Group & Community Revenue System**

What they get:

- **Area Audit** — why your market will or will not support group revenue
- **Partner Map** — real orgs, named contacts, priority order for outreach
- **Playbook** — offers, scripts, follow-up cadence, tracker, numbers to watch
- **Strategy web page** — all three documents delivered as a page your team can open and use
- **Implementation support** — 2 weeks of help for your staff getting started

**Terms:** 50% to start. 50% on handoff of the strategy page.

**Not included:** Ongoing support after the two-week window, or updated research.

**Research note:** Findings reflect your market at delivery. Local conditions and contacts can change — deliverables are not monitored or updated after that.

**Outcome:** Fill slow dayparts with recurring local groups.

This is what the page exists to sell. Lead with this on every call.

---

## Upsell — website / redesign add-on — $3,000–$7,000

After they buy the core, or on the same call if they ask:

The public face of the group booking machine — booking path, group CTA, handouts that match the outreach.

**Included light (core clients with an existing site):** One group-booking landing section and CTA wiring.

**Paid add-on:** Full redesign as a package add, not a separate identity.

Same brand. Same CTA. Same sales call.

---

## Downsell — Audit + Partner Map only — $2,000

If they flinch on $5k:

- Area Audit + Partner Map only
- No Playbook, no strategy page
- Paid in full before work begins

Same shape as the full deliverable. Smaller scope. Still cash-positive.

## Continuity (later — not day one)

Monthly "outreach ops" or site support after delivery. Park until you have 3 paid cores.

Last-resort close on the call, not the storefront headline. If someone only wants a website, that is a downsell after they decline the system — or a separate quiet page later. Do not rebuild the homepage around them.
